Your website — 4 short steps

About 10 minutes total. I'll handle the rest.
Nothing here needs any technical knowledge.

1

Create your Vercel account

about 2 minutes

  1. On your computer, go to vercel.com
  2. Click Sign Up
  3. Choose "Continue with Email" — not Google, not GitHub
  4. Enter your email and a password, then click the confirmation link in your inbox

Use your own email — this account belongs to you, and only you.

2

Upgrade to Pro

about 2 minutes

  1. Click your picture in the top-right corner
  2. Settings → Billing
  3. Choose Pro ($20 per month) and enter your card

Your website is for a business, and the free plan is only for personal use, so this step keeps everything above board.

3

Create a deployment key

about 2 minutes

This key is what lets me upload updates to your site for you.

  1. Go to vercel.com/account/tokens
  2. Click Create Token
  3. Name it: burgonomics-deploy
  4. Expiration: choose "No Expiration"
  5. Click Create, then copy the key — it only appears once

Then send me two things:

  • The key you just copied
  • Your team name — it's the word right after vercel.com/ in your browser address bar, or under Settings → General
About that key: it only lets me publish updates. It can't change your billing, your domain, or your password. You can switch it off at any time from that same page (just click Delete) and the website will keep working — you'd just need to send me a new key next time you want an update.
4

Point your domain at the website

about 5 minutes · do this after I've uploaded the site

Once I've uploaded the site, Vercel will show you two specific settings to copy across. Here's exactly where to put them in GoDaddy:

  1. Sign in at godaddy.com
  2. Click your name, top-right → My Products
  3. Next to your domain, click DNS (or "Manage DNS")
  4. You'll see a table of settings. At the top it says "Nameservers" — tell me what it says before you change anything
  5. Find the row where Type is A and Name is @
    → click the pencil icon → change the Value to 76.76.21.21
    → if there's no such row, click Add and create one
  6. Find the row where Type is CNAME and Name is www
    → change the Value to cname.vercel-dns.com
    → if there's no such row, click Add and create one
  7. If you see anything labelled Forwarding, delete it — it overrides everything
  8. Click Save
  9. Take a screenshot and send it to me so I can check it's right
If the values on your Vercel page differ from the ones above, use Vercel's. They're the official ones for your account.
That's it.
After that I'll check everything is working. The site will be live on your own domain, and the account and domain stay firmly in your name.
